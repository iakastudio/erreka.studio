Raised by the raw landscapes of the Atlantic coast and the Pyrenees, I'm deeply inspired by the natural world, oscillating between its constant movement and the seemingly still. I make images that aim to be timeless.

Stories told, not invented. The beauty of the moment is often enough.

Years in front of the lens taught me what it feels like to sometimes be reduced to an image. I photograph people differently because of it, not looking for the pose, I'm looking for the moment just before or just after. The laugh that escapes, the look that reveals more than intended. The real person, not the one they prepared for the camera.

More than documentary photography, I look to capture a feeling, a texture, something that stays.

There is beauty in patina. That's what I look for.
